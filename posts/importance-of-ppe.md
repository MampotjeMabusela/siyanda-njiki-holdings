# Why PPE Matters

In high-risk environments such as construction sites, electrical maintenance zones, and transport depots, personal protective equipment (PPE) is a frontline control that saves lives. Effective PPE use reduces injuries, supports legal compliance, and strengthens team confidence.

PPE should be selected according to actual site hazards, not generic assumptions. Hard hats, gloves, eye protection, safety footwear, and high-visibility clothing must match task requirements and be regularly inspected for wear.

## Building a stronger PPE culture

- Conduct hazard-based PPE assessments before work begins.
- Issue correct sizes and enforce proper fit for all personnel.
- Replace damaged or expired PPE immediately.
- Train teams continuously on why PPE matters, not just how to wear it.

## South African compliance and performance

Workplace safety regulations in South Africa require employers to provide and maintain suitable protective equipment. Beyond compliance, organizations with strong PPE discipline experience fewer incidents, less downtime, and better workforce morale.
