# Supply Chain Management

For service businesses in South Africa, a strong supply chain is often the difference between delivering on schedule and missing key deadlines. Procurement delays, stock gaps, and poor vendor coordination can affect every part of a project lifecycle.

At Syanda Njiki Holdings, supply planning starts with understanding real site demand. Teams forecast materials by phase, verify supplier lead times, and maintain clear reorder points for critical items such as PPE, electrical components, and consumables.

## Core practices that improve supply performance

- Build relationships with reliable local suppliers and alternate backup vendors.
- Standardize frequently used items to simplify ordering and reduce waste.
- Track stock movement weekly to avoid overbuying and emergency procurement.
- Align procurement timelines with project milestones and transport availability.

## Why it matters for South African operations

Regional delivery times can vary, and supply chains can be affected by fuel costs, route disruptions, and import dependency. Businesses that plan proactively are better positioned to keep projects moving even when external conditions change.

Operational excellence is not only about doing work well on-site; it is also about getting the right materials, at the right quality, at the right time. A disciplined supply chain protects margins, improves customer confidence, and supports repeat business.
